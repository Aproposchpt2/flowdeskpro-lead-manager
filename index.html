How It Works

A system that thinks for itself.

FlowDesk Pro is not software you operate — it is intelligence you deploy. The moment a call comes in, the system identifies the caller, understands their intent, captures every detail, and sets the next action before the call ends.

  • Caller recognition: Returning callers greeted by name — "Welcome back, Jeffrey"
  • Intent routing: Routes by what the caller says, not what menu they press
  • Automatic record creation: Contact, call record, lead, task — all created simultaneously
  • Follow-up sequences: SMS and email sent at the right time, automatically
  • Real-time dashboard: Every record appears the moment the call ends
